# 09 · Bibliography (annotated, keyed to decisions)

Entries give what the library takes from each source. Venues are omitted where not
certain; the M0 agent should complete them in the forest.

## Virtual double categories and formal category theory
- **Cruttwell, Shulman** — *A unified framework for generalized multicategories* (TAC 2010).
  Virtual double categories, monads and modules, `Mod`, virtual equipments, `Mod`
  preserves equipments; monads `T` on a virtual equipment, the horizontal Kleisli
  construction, `T`-monoids as monoids in it, `KMod(T, X)` an equipment. Source for
  D-RT-09, D-FT-01, D-SP-09, AT-FT-1, AT-FT-3, AT-SP-13.
- **Koudenburg** — *Augmented virtual double categories* (TAC 2020); *Formal category
  theory in augmented virtual double categories* (arXiv, 2020s). Augmentation,
  Yoneda embeddings and formal category theory in AVDCs. Source for D-CH-05
  (augmented), D-FT-01, D-FT-03 (2), OQ-FT-1.
- **Leinster** — *Higher Operads, Higher Categories* (CUP 2004). fc-multicategories,
  generalized multicategories, free T-multicategories. Background for D-KR-06.
- **Riehl, Verity** — *Elements of ∞-Category Theory* (CUP 2022); *Kan extensions and
  the calculus of modules for ∞-categories* (AGT 2017). Homotopy 2-category, virtual
  equipment of modules, absolute liftings. Source for D-FT-04, D-FT-05, D-FT-03 (8).
- **Ruit** — *Formal category theory in ∞-equipments I, II* (arXiv 2023–24).
  Double ∞-categories, ∞-equipments, companions/conjoints. Comparison target AT-FT-4.
- **Haugseng** — *Bimodules and natural transformations for enriched ∞-categories*
  (HHA 2016). Double ∞-category of enriched ∞-categories and bimodules. Comparison
  target for `Mod(Mat Kan)`.
- **Street, Walters** — *Yoneda structures on 2-categories* (J. Algebra 1978).
  Admissibility as a size device; background for D-RT-12.
- **Hermida** — *Representable multicategories* (Adv. Math. 2000). Monoidal = representable. D-CH-03.

## Shapes, nerves, patterns
- **Weber** — *Familial 2-functors and parametric right adjoints* (TAC 2007). `Θ_T`,
  generic/free factorization, nerve theorem. Source for D-KR-03, D-KR-04.
- **Berger, Melliès, Weber** — *Monads with arities and their associated theories*
  (JPAA 2012). The general nerve theorem formalized as AT-KR-2. OQ-KR-1 (symmetric case).
- **Chu, Haugseng** — *Homotopy-coherent algebra via Segal conditions* (Adv. Math. 2021).
  Algebraic patterns, Segal objects, comparison of Segal-space and fibration styles.
  Source for D-KR-04, D-CH-05 (rejected style A), AT-RT-9.
- **Gepner, Haugseng** — *Enriched ∞-categories via non-symmetric ∞-operads*
  (Adv. Math. 2015). `Δ_X`, categorical algebras, generalized non-symmetric ∞-operads
  as the ∞-analogue of virtual double categories. Source for D-KR-07, AT-SP-3, D-SP-07.
- **Moerdijk, Weiss** — *Dendroidal sets* (AGT 2007). `Ω`, `Ω_p`. D-KR-03.
- **Kock** — *Polynomial functors and trees* (IMRN 2011). Alternative definition of Ω. D-KR-03 fallback.
- **Cisinski, Moerdijk** — *Dendroidal Segal spaces and ∞-operads* (J. Topol. 2013). D-SP-06.
- **Ara, Cisinski, Moerdijk** — *The dendroidal category is a test category* (2019). M7.
- **Heuts, Hinich, Moerdijk** — *On the equivalence between Lurie's model and the
  dendroidal model for infinity-operads* (Adv. Math. 2016). D-SP-08 template for style comparisons.
- **Berger** — *Iterated wreath product of the simplex category and iterated loop
  spaces* (Adv. Math. 2007). `Θ_n = Δ ≀ Θ_{n−1}`. D-KR-08.
- **Bergner, Rezk** — *Reedy categories and the Θ-construction* (Math. Z. 2013).
  Elegant Reedy categories. D-KR-05.
- **Berger, Moerdijk** — *On an extension of the notion of Reedy category* (Math. Z.
  2011). Generalized Reedy, EZ categories, normal monomorphisms. D-KR-05, OQ-KR-3.
- **Grandis, Mauri** — *Cubical sets and their site* (TAC 2003). PRO presentation of cubes. D-KR-09.
- **Brown, Higgins** — *On the algebra of cubes* (JPAA 1981); **Brown, Mosa** — *Double
  categories, 2-categories, thin structures and connections* (TAC 1999); **Grandis,
  Paré** — *Adjoint for double categories* (CTGDC 2004). Connections as
  companion/conjoint squares. D-KR-09.

## Homotopy theory and models
- **Simpson** — *Homotopy Theory of Higher Categories* (CUP 2011). Segal
  (n-)categories with discrete object sets, fibrant replacement preserving objects,
  internal homs, limits and colimits in `nCAT`. The Segal-native source for the closure
  package (D-CH-13) and for the mountain (D-SP-08, AT-SP-12).
- **Dwyer, Kan** — *Simplicial localizations of categories*; *Function complexes in
  homotopical algebra* (1980). Rigidification of Segal categories by simplicial
  categories, derived mapping spaces. AT-SP-8, D-RT-08.
- **Hirschhorn** — *Model Categories and Their Localizations* (AMS 2003). Homotopy
  (co)limits, Reedy categories. D-KR-05, AT-SP-12.
- **Barwick** — *Spectral Mackey functors and equivariant algebraic K-theory (I)*
  (Adv. Math. 2017); **Haugseng** — *Iterated spans and classical topological field
  theories* (Math. Z. 2018). Spans via twisted-arrow-type shapes with pullbacks as a
  property; the Segal condition for `Span`. D-SP-05, D-KR-08.
- **Rasekh** — *Yoneda lemma for simplicial spaces* (2017/2021). The universe as a
  complete Segal space via left fibrations; the fibrant-by-construction option. OQ-SP-3.
- **Rezk** — *A model for the homotopy theory of homotopy theory* (TAMS 2001). Segal
  spaces, completeness (rejected at the root, used for `Mod(Span Kan)`). D-SP-05.
- **Hirschowitz, Simpson** — *Descente pour les n-champs* (arXiv 1998). Segal categories. D-CH-05.
- **Bergner** — *A model category structure on the category of simplicial categories*
  (TAMS 2007); *Three models for the homotopy theory of homotopy theories* (Topology
  2007). Fibrant replacement for Segal categories, Segal categories ≃ CSS. D-KR-11, AT-SP-5.
- **Joyal, Tierney** — *Quasi-categories vs Segal spaces* (2007). D-SP-08 (optional).
- **Cisinski** — *Higher Categories and Homotopical Algebra* (CUP 2019). Universe of
  left fibrations, `S`, minimal use of model structures. D-SP-01, D-SP-02, D-SP-08.
- **Kapulkin, Lumsdaine** — *The simplicial model of univalent foundations (after
  Voevodsky)* (JEMS 2021). The universe `U` is a Kan complex. D-SP-01, AT-SP-1.
- **Sattler** — *The equivalence extension property and model structures* (arXiv 2017).
  Alternative fibrancy proof for universes. OQ-SP-2.
- **Doherty, Kapulkin, Lindsey, Sattler** — *Cubical models of (∞,1)-categories*
  (Mem. AMS 2024). Cubical fibrant-object models. D-KR-09.
- **Lurie** — *Higher Topos Theory* (2009); *Kerodon*. Slices via joins, limits as
  terminal objects of slices, cocartesian edges via trivial fibrations. D-UP-03, D-UP-04.
- **Garner** — *Understanding the small object argument* (ACS 2009). Algebraic SOA
  option for D-KR-11.

## Operads and `E_n`
- **Haugseng** — *∞-operads via symmetric sequences* (Math. Z. 2022). ∞-operads as
  associative algebras in symmetric sequences under the composition product. D-SP-09, AT-SP-10.
- **Fiedorowicz** — *The symmetric bar construction* (preprint); *Braided operads*.
  Braided operads; braided operads with contractible components present `E_2`; the
  operad in groupoids with objects `Σ_n` and morphisms braids. D-SP-09, AT-SP-11.
- **Berger** — *Combinatorial models for real configuration spaces and E_n-operads*
  (Contemp. Math. 1997). The complete-graph operads `K_n`, symmetric operads in finite
  posets with `|K_n(k)| ≃ Conf_k(ℝ^n)`. D-SP-09 (`E_n`, finite `n ≥ 3`).
- **Smith** — *Simplicial group models for Ω^n S^n X* (Israel J. Math. 1994). The
  filtration of the Barratt–Eccles operad by `E_n` suboperads. D-SP-09 alternative.
- **Zhang** — *Group operads and homotopy theory* (arXiv 2011); **Gurski** — *Operads,
  tensor products, and the categorical Borel construction* (arXiv 2015). Action operads
  `G` and the monads `T_G`; symmetric, braided, ribbon, cactus cases uniformly. D-SP-09.
- **May** — *The Geometry of Iterated Loop Spaces* (1972). Little cubes; the reference
  meaning of `E_n`. D-SP-09.

## Type theory and displayed structures
- **Ahrens, Lumsdaine** — *Displayed categories* (LMCS 2019). Families over spans; the
  1-level precedent for D-RT-10's "families" choice.
- **New, Licata** — *A formal logic for formal category theory* (2023). Virtual
  equipment type theory; not implemented, used as a spec for D-FT-03.
